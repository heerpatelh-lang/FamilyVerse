# FAMILYVERSE — Game Rules & Specifications

**Competition**: 6th Annual COE STEM Programming with AI Competition  
**Track**: Cycle 2 — Grades 6–8  
**Team**: 467 | **Team Code**: JFXGN  
**Core Tagline**: *"One Family. Five Games. Infinite Possibilities."*

---

## Overview

FAMILYVERSE provides five distinct, culturally rich, and fully playable family games. The host acts as the game master, leading sessions, toggling timers, revealing AI content, and logging verified scores.

---

### Game 1: Football Frenzy
- **Game Type**: Team Competition (2 Teams)
- **Concept**: A high-energy stadium showdown where teams alternate tackling tactical football scenarios, global football trivia, and match bonus missions.
- **Rounds**: Configurable (Default: 3 rounds per team).
- **Turn Flow**:
  1. Team is selected or rotated.
  2. Match timer begins (Default: 45 seconds per turn).
  3. AI tactical scenario, trivia question, or bonus mission is presented to the team.
  4. Team deliberates aloud and gives their answer.
  5. Host reveals the verified answer and explanation.
  6. Host awards standard points or registers goals / bonus points using dedicated controls.
- **Scoring**:
  - Regular goal: 1 Goal (100 pts)
  - Trivia correct: 50 pts
  - Bonus mission completed: 75 pts
  - Incorrect: 0 pts (No penalty unless penalty mode enabled: -25 pts)
- **Tie-Breaker**: "Golden Goal Sudden Death" round featuring an ultra-challenging tactical decision scenario.

---

### Game 2: Heritage Quest
- **Game Type**: Individual Player Challenge
- **Concept**: An inspiring expedition through UAE national heritage, Arabic traditions, architecture, historical achievements, and global cultural intersections.
- **Categories**: Traditional Objects, Landmarks & Architecture, Inventions & Science, Customs & Traditions.
- **Difficulty Levels**:
  - *Easy*: Foundational traditions and famous landmarks (50 pts, 45s timer)
  - *Medium*: Historical nuances, heritage vocabulary, craftsmanship (100 pts, 35s timer)
  - *Hard*: In-depth historical dates, architectural specifics, poetry & seafaring navigation (150 pts, 25s timer)
- **Turn Flow**:
  1. Active player takes the stage.
  2. Category and difficulty are chosen or randomly served by AI.
  3. AI challenge card is revealed (Riddle or Historical Inquiry).
  4. Player speaks answer; Host reveals key.
  5. Host confirms: Correct (+pts), Incorrect (0 pts), or Skip (0 pts, moves to next player).
- **Tie-Breaker**: 3-question rapid-fire cultural sprint.

---

### Game 3: Desert Decision
- **Game Type**: Team Adventure Strategy
- **Concept**: Teams roleplay as desert explorers navigating fictional, age-appropriate expeditions through Arabian sand dunes, oases, and mountain wadis.
- **Gameplay Structure**:
  1. AI generates a rich adventure dilemma (Navigation, Resource Allocation, Weather Survival, Team Camaraderie).
  2. Four strategic action choices (A, B, C, D) are presented on interactive cards.
  3. Team selects an action card.
  4. Host views the comprehensive rubric outlining point distribution (Optimal: 100 pts, Decent: 60 pts, Risky: 30 pts, Counter-productive: 10 pts).
  5. Host reveals the tactical explanation and confirms the awarded points.
- **Safety Note**: All scenarios are strictly fictional survival and teamwork puzzles suitable for children, with zero real-world emergency guidance claims.

---

### Game 4: Festival Food Detective
- **Game Type**: Individual Culinary Investigation
- **Concept**: Players become culinary sleuths deciphering festive recipes, spices, traditional cooking techniques, and multicultural feast customs.
- **Features**:
  - AI riddles spotlighting ingredients, origin lore, and celebration contexts (e.g., Eid feasts, harvest celebrations, national days).
  - Rich culinary illustration cards.
  - Multi-part clue reveal (Clue 1, Clue 2, Final Reveal).
  - Cultural context modal explaining the heritage significance of the culinary dish.
- **Scoring**:
  - Solved on Clue 1: 120 pts
  - Solved on Clue 2: 80 pts
  - Solved on Clue 3: 50 pts
  - Incorrect: 0 pts

---

### Game 5: Sports Strategy Showdown
- **Game Type**: Team Tactical Analysis
- **Concept**: Multi-sport tactical war-room where teams analyze crunch-time scenarios across basketball, football, tennis, athletics, and volleyball.
- **Format**:
  - Scenario card detailing the sports situation (time left, scoreline, team fatigue, defensive setup).
  - Three to four tactical plays with risk vs. reward profiles.
  - Transparent scoring rubric visible to the host with tactical rationale.
  - Host-confirmed point awards and progression.
- **Compliance**: Uses fictional teams and athletes with no real-world player likenesses or trademark infringement.
